## 2. About (Summary) — 2,600 characters max

Copy, trim, or adapt this. Write in first person (“I”) and keep it clear and concrete.

---

I build backend systems that power AI in education.

Right now I work on **1ne.ai** — an AI-powered teacher assistant. My focus is the API and data pipeline: FastAPI, Python 3.11+, PostgreSQL, and SQLAlchemy. I implement auth (JWT, roles, invites), content ingestion (PDF/DOCX upload, OCR, chunking, embeddings), and RAG over pgvector so teachers can search curriculum and generate worksheets from their own materials.

I integrate multiple LLMs (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google) and care about clean APIs, migrations (Alembic), testing, and deployment. The backend runs on Railway and serves a frontend on Vercel.

I’m interested in EdTech, AI/ML, and building tools that scale for teachers and students globally. I’m open to connecting with people in backend engineering, AI, and education technology worldwide.

## 7. Content Ideas to Grow Your Network Globally

Posting consistently matters more than perfection. Ideas tied to your project:

- **“What we’re building”** — 2–3 sentences on 1ne.ai and why it matters for teachers.
- **“Tech choices”** — Why FastAPI, why pgvector, why multi-LLM (short, honest reasons).
- **“Lessons learned”** — One thing that was hard (e.g. OCR, chunking, or migrations) and how you solved it.
- **“Stack in one image”** — Simple diagram or list: Python, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, Redis, LLMs, etc.
- **“Small win”** — e.g. “Shipped RAG pipeline for worksheet generation this week.”
- **“Ask the network”** — Question for other backend or EdTech people (e.g. “How are you handling document processing at scale?”).

Aim for **1–2 posts per week**. Mix project updates, learnings, and questions.
